# MWC Netherite

Modern Warfare Cubed ([MWC](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/modern-warfare-cubed)) just got its props ported to 1.16.5!

## What's Included

MWC Netherite currently includes a wide variety of props ported from Modern Warfare Cubed, including:

* **Electronics & Office**
    * TV
    * Ceiling-mounted TV
    * PC
    * Laptop
    * Desks (multiple variants)
    * Chairs (Home, Office, and Outdoor)
        * Chairs can also be sat on!
* **Street, Military & Medical**
    * Barriers
    * Caution Sign
    * SWAT Truck
    * Sandbags
    * Sandbag Walls
    * Operating Tables (multiple variants)
* **Gravestones**
    * Standard Gravestone
    * Cross Gravestones (multiple variants)
    * Skull Gravestone
* **Containers & Appliances**
    * Ammunition Box
    * Barrels
    * Dumpsters
    * Trash Bins
    * Fridges (Open and Closed)
    * Electric Boxes (Open and Closed)
    * Filing Cabinets (Open and Closed)
    * Vending Machines
    * Wooden Crates (Open and Closed)
* **Weapon Storage**
    * Russian Weapon Crate
    * Weapon Cases (Large and Small)
    * Weapon Lockers
* **Ventilation**
    * Vents
    * Corner Vents
    * Vent Ends
    * Vent Intersections
* **Lighting**
    * Ceiling Lamps (multiple variants)
    * Floodlights
    * Dual Floodlights
    * Towable Floodlights
* **Storage & Infrastructure**
    * Shelves (Left, Right, and Top)
    * Server Racks (multiple variants)
    * Lockers
    * SCP Lockers
* **Other Props**
    * Body Bags
    * Hanging Bodies
    * Impaled Bodies
    * Forklifts (multiple variants)
    * Spooky Scarecrow
    * Spooky Ghost

Plans are to port over every single prop from MWC, but be patient because this takes time!

## What Won't Be Included

*   Weapons from MWC
    *   MWC still needs some major improvements before we can even consider porting.
